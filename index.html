Dujian Ding

Research Scientist · Meta Superintelligence Lab

I am a research scientist at Meta Superintelligence Lab, building the data flywheel for coding agents. My work focuses on usability and self-improvement: making agents easier to work with and enabling them to improve themselves. I contribute to the Muse model series, including Muse Spark and Muse Wonder.

I completed my PhD at the University of British Columbia (UBC), advised by Professor Laks V.S. Lakshmanan. My doctoral research focused on making AI more efficient and cost-effective through large language model routing, adaptive inference, and data and model management.

Previously, I earned bachelor's degrees from Simon Fraser University (SFU) and Zhejiang University, and spent eight months working with Professor Martin Ester on tumor detection.

I see three forms of self-reproduction—cells, capitalism, and AI—each nourishing the next.
